About

I am a Ph.D. student in ECE at UC Santa Barbara, advised by Prof. Zheng Zhang. I work on memory- and energy-efficient training.

My core direction is back-propagation-free (BP-free) training: gradients are estimated from forward passes alone (zeroth-order optimization), which makes on-device training a plug-in over an inference accelerator: a training engine needs little more than the inference engine already on the chip. I apply it to:

  • MCUFull-model quantized training on microcontrollers with 256 KB–1 MB SRAM. Poor Man's Training on MCUs (TODAES 2025)
  • LLMLow-bit (INT8/INT4) zeroth-order fine-tuning of large language models. QuZO (EMNLP 2025)
  • OpticalOn-chip training of physics-informed neural networks on silicon photonics. Scalable BP-free Optical PINN (TODAES 2026) · On-chip PDE solver demo (OFC 2025)

I also work on efficient LLM fine-tuning and optimizers (FuRA, Muon²), and have interned at Amazon Lab126 and Hewlett Packard Labs.

FuRA: full-rank PEFT

TL;DR LoRA-level cost, Full-FT-level performance (+1.37 over Full FT on LLaMA-3-8B). Each layer is factored as W = L·S·R with L fixed to the pretrained SVD basis; training only S and R gives full-rank, spectrally preconditioned updates.

BP-free training on MCUs

TL;DR Add a control unit and repeated forward passes to a quantized inference engine and it becomes a training engine. Full-model training fits in 1 MB SRAM (sparse: 256 KB) and matches BP accuracy.

BP-free optical PINN training

TL;DR A sparse-grid Stein estimator removes BP from the PINN loss, and tensor-train ZO training keeps the search space small. Together they enable real-size PINN training on photonic tensor cores.
